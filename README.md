# Marvel-Crossover-Legacy
MARVEL: CROSSOVER LEGACY

The Ultimate Marvel & Capcom Arcade Fighting Collection

Developed by Yamanatsuchi Initiative | Windows PC

MARVEL: CROSSOVER LEGACY is a fan-made arcade fighting game collection that brings together eight classic fighting games from the golden age of Marvel and Capcom crossovers.

Experience the evolution of legendary arcade battles, from X-Men: Children of the Atom to Marvel vs. Capcom 2: New Age of Heroes, alongside two hidden classics from the Street Fighter EX series.

Built with a passion for retro gaming and video game preservation, Marvel: Crossover Legacy aims to deliver an authentic arcade experience with modern collection features while preserving the original gameplay, graphics, music, and mechanics.

Featured Games

Marvel Arcade Collection

1. X-Men: Children of the Atom (1994)
2. Marvel Super Heroes (1995)
3. X-Men vs. Street Fighter (1996)
4. Marvel Super Heroes vs. Street Fighter (1997)
5. Marvel vs. Capcom: Clash of Super Heroes (1998)
6. Marvel vs. Capcom 2: New Age of Heroes (2000)

Secret CHEAT EX Collection
7. Street Fighter EX Plus (1997)
8. Street Fighter EX2 Plus (1999)

Bonus: Street Fighter EX3 is featured exclusively in the Arcade Jukebox.

Features

- Authentic Arcade Experience — Original arcade gameplay and presentation.
- Interactive Game Selection — Animated game cards, official logos, a rotating Earth background, and authentic gameplay previews.
- 108 Planned Achievements — Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum challenges, featuring game-specific notification sounds.
- Unlockable Character Gallery — Explore character biographies, original artwork, portraits, and game histories.
- Arcade Jukebox — Enjoy soundtracks from all eight playable games, plus Street Fighter EX3.
- Secret Cheat Menus — Discover hidden features through classic button combinations.
- CHEAT EX — Unlock two additional Street Fighter EX arcade games with dedicated cheat options.
- Customizable Controls — Keyboard and controller configuration.
- Custom Pause System — Convenient pause, resume, and return-to-selection functionality.
- Original Assets — Authentic game music, artwork, and gameplay footage, without AI-generated replacement content.

CHEAT EX

Unlock the hidden Street Fighter EX collection and access special gameplay options, including:

- Infinite Health
- Infinite Power
- Infinite Time
- Unlock All Secret Characters

Each game features independent cheat settings.

Achievements & Unlockables

Complete challenges, defeat powerful bosses, master combos, and conquer Arcade Mode to earn achievements.

Complete any game for the first time to unlock the collection's character gallery, featuring artwork, biographies, and historical information.

Development

Platform: Windows PC
Language: C++17
Development Environment: Visual Studio 2022
Arcade Systems: Capcom CPS-2, Sega NAOMI, Capcom ZN-1, and ZN-2
